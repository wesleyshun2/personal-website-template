Minimalism is not about what is missing, but about what is kept. In this post, we explore the principles of minimalist design in the digital age.

## Why Minimalism?

1. **Focus**: Less noise means more focus on the content.
2. **Performance**: Fewer elements often lead to faster load times.
3. **Elegance**: A clean design feels premium and professional.
